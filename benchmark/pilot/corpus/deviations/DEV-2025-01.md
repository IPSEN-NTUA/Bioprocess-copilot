# DEV-2025-01: Feed pump over-delivery

_SYNTHETIC BENCHMARK DATA - generated by a process simulator for a fictional lab (Larkspur Bio). Not real measurements._

**Author:** Elena Varga  
**Date:** 2025-03-26

**Affected:** FB-005 (R2)

## Description
Feed balance at the end of FB-005 showed 314 mL consumed while the pump counter gave 273 mL.

## Root cause
Worn pump tubing on the R2 feed pump; the pump delivered about 15 % more than its setpoint.

## Impact on data
FB-005 received more glucose and xylose than planned. Exclude FB-005 from the strain comparison.

## Corrective action
Tubing replaced and pump recalibrated (maintenance log 2025-03-26). Repeat run FB-008.
