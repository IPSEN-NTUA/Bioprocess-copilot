# SOP-FB-001 Xylitol fed-batch in 1 L bench reactors (version 3)

_SYNTHETIC BENCHMARK DATA - generated by a process simulator for a fictional lab (Larkspur Bio). Not real measurements._

**Author:** Elena Varga  
**Date:** 2025-01-15

## Batch phase
300 mL YP + 20 g/L glucose, 30 C, pH 5.5 controlled with 2 M KOH / 1 M H2SO4, 900 rpm, 0.7 L/min air. Inoculate from an overnight YPD preculture to OD600 2.

## Fed phase
Start the feed at 7.5 h (normally just after glucose depletion) at 2.0 mL/h. Feed A: 100 g/L glucose + 300 g/L xylose. C5 feeds: C5 fraction diluted with water, glucose supplemented to 30 g/L, xylose topped up to 140 g/L. Feed B (P6 only): 150 g/L glucose + 300 g/L xylose.

Feed bottles stand on the feed balance; feed added = balance loss / density. Measured densities: Feed A 1.15 g/mL, Feed B 1.17 g/mL, C5 feeds 1.09 g/mL.

## Sampling
7 mL at 0, 4, 8, 23.5, 30, 47.5, 72, 96, 120 and 143.5 h (167.5 h for C5 runs). OD600, dry weight (3 x 2 mL) and HPLC (SOP-ANA-003).

## Antifoam
Only if foam reaches the headplate; record the amount.
