# SOP-ANA-003 HPLC analysis of fermentation samples (version 2)

## Scope
Quantification of glucose, xylose, arabinose, xylitol, glycerol, acetate, ethanol, formate, pyruvate, succinate, furfural, hmf in culture supernatants.

## Method
- Column: organic acid column, 0.6 mL/min 5 mM H2SO4, 55 C.
- Detection: refractive index + UV 280 nm for furfural and HMF.
- Samples centrifuged, supernatant frozen at -20 C, analysed in batches on Tuesdays and Fridays.
- Dilute so that every analyte is below 10 g/L in the vial (1:1 to 1:50).
- Five-point external calibration per sequence; check standard every 10 injections.
- Report values below the LOD as '<LOD' (LOD 0.02-0.05 g/L in the vial).

## Changes from version 1
- Succinate, furfural and HMF added. Values for these analytes before June 2025 are not available.
